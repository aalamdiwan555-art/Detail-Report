Explaining admin panel access
To access the admin panel:

Open the app’s login screen, or the main screen after logging in.
Press and hold the ULTRA logo for 6 seconds.
The Administrator access dialog will appear.
Enter the provisioned administrator email and password.
Tap OPEN ADMIN.
Admin access does not work through the regular Login or Sign Up fields. It requires the administrator credentials configured at build time. If the credentials were not configured in the APK, the admin panel cannot be opened.
